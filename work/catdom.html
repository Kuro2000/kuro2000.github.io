The setup

XGame is a mid-sized Vietnamese game company, around 200 people, taking its first steps into hybrid-casual. I joined as Senior Game Designer to bring big-studio fundamentals into that transition: design process, documentation standards, and AI-assisted workflows.

Catdom: Color Hole was already live when I arrived. Inheriting a shipped game is a different discipline from concepting one. The audience exists, the economy is running, and a bad level curve shows up in tomorrow’s retention numbers.

What I own

  • The improvement backlog, prioritized together with the product owner
  • Level curve design: difficulty, pacing, and progression across the live content pipeline
  • Assigning and facilitating a five-designer team on content and features
  • Hosting the recurring testing and level-tuning sessions

The process behind the game

The habits from this title became the studio’s habits. The documentation and workflow standards I wrote for level and content management, project workflows, ideation and kick-off were adopted company-wide, across roughly ten projects in 2026.

The part I’m proudest of: an AI-assisted pipeline built on Claude that generates level candidates and automates our metrics reports. It’s not a demo. The GD team runs on it daily.
