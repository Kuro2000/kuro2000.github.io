Trix for MENA

Trix is a card game every Saudi family already knows. The job was moving it from the living-room table onto phones for the MENA market, without losing what makes it Trix.

Designing across a culture

I’m a Vietnamese designer who had never played Trix at a family gathering. That gap was the design problem. We closed it with cultural research, an Arabic-first approach to every screen, and direct collaboration with a MENA local consultant on everything from card feel to character design.

  • Researched how Trix is actually played: house rules, table talk, what a “proper” match feels like
  • Designed UI/UX Arabic-first, right-to-left from the start rather than mirrored as an afterthought
  • Ideated the game’s IP characters together with the local consultant, iterating until they read as familiar rather than foreign

From table to phone

The core rules came from the physical game, and faithfulness mattered more than novelty. My design work went into game feel and the social layer around the table.

  • Tuned scoring and card-effect feedback so digital play keeps the table’s rhythm
  • Designed the social side: waiting rooms, channels, customization, the spaces between matches

Running it live

After launch I operated the game’s monetization inside VNG’s live-ops framework: starter offers, dynamic offers, journey offers, and shop bundles, plus the premium currency store and in-game events. The same toolkit later carried over to other live card titles I supported.

Outcome

4.7★ from 797 ratings, and a Top-3 Card game on Google Play MENA in launch week. The reviews that mattered most were the ones saying it felt like the real thing.
