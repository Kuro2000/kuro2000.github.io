About me

I’m Duc Thang. For the last four years I’ve designed mobile games across hybrid-casual puzzles, card, music, and social genres for many companies: VNG, Amanotes, and most recently XGame Studio. I have experience working on every aspect of the product, from the first concept, the game content (levels & blockers) and the economy underneath it to monetization, live-ops, and building the work process for teams that keeps a game growing.

  • Concept & ideation Game concepts and feature designs, from raw ideas to logical game mechanics
  • AI-assisted design workflows Claude and Gemini, worked into the design loop.
  • System design Systems and mechanics, economy design, level design in 2D and 3D.
  • Mentoring Experienced leading Game Designers in projects; ran XGame’s GD internship program end-to-end.
